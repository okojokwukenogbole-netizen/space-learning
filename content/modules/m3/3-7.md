# Lesson 3.7 — STEM Pipeline: Catch-The-Young Initiatives

**Module:** M3 — Space Economy, Capacity Building & Human Capital
**Estimated Time:** 8 minutes

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Design** primary and secondary school space inspire programs.
2. **Establish** space clubs and specialized STEM pipelines.
3. **Evaluate** the long-term impact of youth engagement.

## 🚀 The Hook
> *An astronaut today was inspired by a rocket launch 30 years ago. The engineers who will build your 2040 satellite are currently in primary school. Catch them young.*

## 📖 Core Content

### 1. Topic 40 in Your Notes
Your notes list: *Catch-The-Young Initiatives — Launching primary and secondary school inspire programs, space clubs, and specialized STEM pipelines to cultivate interest in national space systems.*

### 2. Why Start Young?
- **Neuroplasticity** — Children absorb complex concepts easily.
- **Identity formation** — Careers are shaped by age 12–14.
- **Skills pipeline** — 15-year runway to produce a qualified engineer.
- **Geopolitical resilience** — Reduces brain drain by building local talent.

### 3. Inspire Programs
What works:
- **Rocket clubs** — Model rocketry and safe solid-fuel experiments.
- **CanSat competitions** — Build a soda-can-sized "satellite" that launches on a small rocket.
- **Astronomy nights** — Telescope viewing and sky mapping.
- **Satellite tracking** — Use free software to track real satellites.
- **Hackathons** — Solve real space problems with software.

### 4. Space Clubs
Structured after-school programs with:
- Weekly meetings
- Mentor assignments (university students or professionals)
- Annual competitions
- Field trips to ground stations or labs

### 5. Specialized STEM Pipelines
- **Primary** — Broad inspire programs, space-themed curriculum.
- **Secondary** — Advanced clubs, competitions, coding bootcamps.
- **University** — Degree programs, internships at space agencies.
- **Professional** — Continued training, certifications, fellowships.

### 6. Measuring Impact
- Number of students enrolled in STEM fields.
- Retention rate to university.
- Graduates employed in space sector.
- Long-term: national workforce size.
