# Lesson 5.2 — SAT MDA & Global Watchkeeping

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Define** Satellite Maritime Domain Awareness (SAT MDA).
2. **Evaluate** the role of global watchkeeping.
3. **Integrate** SAT MDA into national security.

## 📖 Core Content
Your notes highlight **SAT MDA** — Satellite Maritime Domain Awareness — as a critical capability for nations with coastlines and maritime economic zones.

SAT MDA enables:
- **Monitoring of vessel traffic** — tracking ships in territorial waters and EEZs.
- **Detection of illegal fishing** — directly linked to your notes on economic security.
- **Watchkeeping** — continuous observation of maritime areas of interest.
- **Global Watchkeeping Alliances** — collaboration with other nations for broader coverage.

For nations like Nigeria, Angola, and other African coastal states, SAT MDA is essential to protecting fisheries, preventing smuggling, and securing offshore energy assets.

## ❓ Check Your Understanding

**Q1.** What does SAT MDA stand for?
A) Satellite Maritime Domain Awareness B) Satellite Missile Defense Agency C) Space and Terrestrial Monitoring Data Analysis D) None of the above
**Answer: A.** Rationale: Your notes define it as "Satellite Maritime Domain Awareness."

**Q2.** What is the goal of global watchkeeping?
A) To monitor global maritime traffic B) To launch satellites C) To build ships D) To fish
**Answer: A.** Rationale: Watchkeeping involves monitoring areas of interest.

**Q3.** Why is SAT MDA important for emerging economies?
A) To protect maritime resources B) To monitor illegal fishing C) To secure trade routes D) All of the above
**Answer: D.** Rationale: Maritime awareness is critical for economic and security reasons.
