# Lesson 5.4 — GNPT Redundancy & Troop Navigation

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Secure** precise tracking infrastructure for personnel deployment.
2. **Establish** system redundancy by multi-linking alternative constellations.
3. **Evaluate** the role of GNPT in military operations.

## 📖 Core Content
Your notes raise a critical operational question: *"Troops Nav?"* — how do you ensure your forces always have precise positioning, even if a single GNSS constellation is denied or degraded?

The answer is **redundancy through multi-linking**. Your notes explicitly list **GPS, Beidou, and Galileo** as alternative constellations to link.

Key principles:
- **Multi-Constellation Receivers** — Devices that receive from GPS, Beidou, and Galileo simultaneously.
- **Differential Network Positioning Techniques (DNPT)** — Ground-based augmentation for centimeter accuracy.
- **No Single Point of Failure** — If one constellation goes down, others continue.
- **Sovereign Backup** — Consideration of independent positioning capabilities.
