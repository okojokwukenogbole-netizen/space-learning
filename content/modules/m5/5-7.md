# Lesson 5.7 — Satellite Communications: Up/Down Links & CCC

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Explain** ground station architecture.
2. **Optimize** uplink and downlink capabilities.
3. **Evaluate** Command, Control, and Communications (CCC) systems.

## 📖 Core Content
Your notes list **"Satellite Coms Up/Down Links / CCC: Ground station architecture, uplink/downlink optimization, and Command, Control, and Communications."**

Every satellite operation depends on the communication link between ground and space:

- **Uplink** — Commands and data sent *to* the satellite.
- **Downlink** — Data and telemetry sent *from* the satellite.
- **Ground Station** — The facility that manages the link.
- **CCC** — The integrated system for Command, Control, and Communications.

Optimization means maximizing bandwidth, minimizing latency, and ensuring the link is secure against jamming or interception.
