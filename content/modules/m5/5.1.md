# Lesson 5.1 — Space Domain Awareness (SDA) & Modular EO Centers

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Define** Space Domain Awareness (SDA).
2. **Manage** a modular EO Center.
3. **Track** satellites and drive local rocketry approval.

## 📖 Core Content
Your notes emphasize **Space Domain Awareness (SDA)** as a foundational capability. SDA means knowing what is happening in orbit — who owns what, where assets are, and what threats exist.

Key components from your notes:
- **Modular EO Center** — A flexible, scalable Earth Observation facility.
- **Battlefield Management** — Integrating space data into tactical operations.
- **Satellite Tracking** — Monitoring Sat 1, 3, 4, 5, and ComSat 2A, 2B.
- **Local Rocketry Approval** — Regulatory pathway for domestic launch.
- **Indigenous AIT** — Assembly, Integration, and Testing capability.

Without SDA, a nation is blind in orbit. With it, space becomes a governed domain rather than a chaotic one.

## ❓ Check Your Understanding

**Q1.** What does SDA stand for?
A) Space Domain Awareness B) Satellite Data Analysis C) Space Defense Agency D) None of the above
**Answer: A.** Rationale: Your notes define it as "Space Domain Awareness."

**Q2.** What is a Modular EO Center?
A) A flexible, scalable Earth Observation center B) A fixed, permanent structure C) A ground station D) A satellite
**Answer: A.** Rationale: "Modular" implies flexibility and scalability.

**Q3.** Why is tracking satellites important?
A) To avoid collisions B) To monitor adversary capabilities C) Both A and B D) Neither A nor B
**Answer: C.** Rationale: Tracking serves both safety and security purposes.
