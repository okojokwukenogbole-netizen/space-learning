Lesson 1.4 — Strategic Independence & Network Autonomy
Learning Objectives:

Define sovereign network autonomy.

Analyze the risks of reliance on foreign space systems (e.g., Starlink blackout).

Design backup plans for operational continuity.

Key Terms: Sovereign Network, Starlink Blackout, Operational Continuity, Backup Plans.

Quiz:

What triggered the "patronise ours" campaign mentioned in your notes?
A) A treaty violation B) Starlink blacked out C) NigeriaSat failed D) A cyber attack
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes state: "Starlink blacked out, patronise ours."

What is the primary goal of sovereign network autonomy?
A) Save money B) Operational continuity in event of foreign system failure C) Increase bandwidth D) Spy on neighbors
Answer: B. Rationale: Backup plans ensure operations continue even if foreign systems fail.

What is the risk of relying solely on foreign space systems?
A) Higher costs B) No risk C) Vulnerability to external control D) Better performance
Answer: C. Rationale: External control can be leveraged politically, as the Starlink example demonstrates.
