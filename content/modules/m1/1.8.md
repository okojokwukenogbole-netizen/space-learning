Lesson 1.8 — The 5–10 Year Strategic Roadmap
Learning Objectives:

Design a concrete 5–10 year strategic roadmap.

Align the roadmap with legal, fiscal, and institutional realities.

Evaluate the roadmap's responsiveness to emerging technologies.

Key Terms: Strategic Roadmap, New Tech Adoption, Institutional Agility.
