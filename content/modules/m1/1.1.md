Lesson 1.1 — Legal & Governance Frameworks
Learning Objectives:

Define the key legal instruments that enable national space activities.

Explain the relationship between space acts, funding mechanisms, and institutional design.

Evaluate how a 5–10 year strategic roadmap aligns with legislative frameworks.

Key Terms: Space Act, Funding Mechanisms, Institutional Restructuring, Strategic Roadmap (5–10 Yr).

Quiz:

Which instrument establishes the legal mandate for space activities?
A) Space Agency Charter B) National Space Act C) Licensing Regime D) Spectrum Management
Answer: B. Rationale: The Space Act is the primary legislation; the Charter establishes the agency itself.

Why is a 5–10 year roadmap critical?
A) To comply with UN treaties B) To ensure political continuity beyond election cycles C) To guarantee launch success D) To reduce satellite costs
Answer: B. Rationale: Long-term roadmaps provide stability against short-term political changes.

What does "institutional restructuring" aim to achieve?
A) Reduce budget B) Adopt new tech easily C) Eliminate private sector D) Centralize all power
Answer: B. Rationale: Restructuring ensures institutions can adapt to new technologies and strategic priorities.
