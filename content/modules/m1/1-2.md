Lesson 1.2 — Outer Space Treaty & Domain Exploitation
Learning Objectives:

Understand the Outer Space Treaty (OST) provisions.

Analyze the tension between OST principles and national domain exploitation.

Evaluate strategies for cyber + space domain integration.

Key Terms: OST, Property Rights, Position Rights, Domain Exploitation, Cyber-Space Nexus.

Quiz:

What does OST primarily prohibit?
A) Commercial space B) National appropriation of celestial bodies C) Satellite launches D) Military communications
Answer: B. Rationale: OST Article II prohibits national appropriation of outer space, including the Moon and other celestial bodies.

What does "domain exploitation" refer to?
A) Exploiting space resources B) Unified policy covering Cyber + Space C) Exploiting foreign nations D) Launching weapons
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes explicitly define it as a unified strategy covering both Cyber and Space dimensions.

Why might a nation strategically avoid certain treaties?
A) They are too expensive B) They restrict national advancement C) They require unanimous consent D) They are outdated
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes state: "strategically avoiding treaties not helpful to national advancements."
