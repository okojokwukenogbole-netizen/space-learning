Lesson 2.8 — Sounding Rockets & H/W-S/W Avionics
Learning Objectives:

Explain the purpose of sounding rockets.

Identify key avionics components.

Evaluate the role of concurrent H/W and S/W development.

Key Terms: Sounding Rockets, Avionics, Telemetry, H/W, S/W.

Quiz:

What is the purpose of sounding rockets?
A) Orbital launches B) Sub-orbital research C) Interplanetary travel D) Satellite deployment
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes describe them as "sub-orbital research rockets."

What does avionics encompass?
A) Telemetry and control systems B) Payload sensors C) Launch vehicle motors D) Ground stations
Answer: A. Rationale: Your notes define it as "Telemetry and control systems."

Why is concurrent H/W and S/W development important?
A) It slows down projects B) It ensures compatibility and reduces integration risk C) It saves money D) It is not important
Answer: B. Rationale: Developing hardware and software concurrently reduces integration risks and ensures compatibility.
