Lesson 2.6 — Satellite Aging, Orbital Gaps & Payload Obsolescence
Learning Objectives:

Analyze satellite lifecycle degradation.

Identify the causes of orbital coverage gaps.

Address payload technical obsolescence.

Key Terms: Sat Aging, Orbital Gaps, Payload Aging, Capability Gaps.

Quiz:

What causes orbital gaps?
A) Too many satellites B) Sat aging and lifecycle degradation C) Ground station failure D) Software updates

What is payload aging?
