# Lesson 3.1 — Capacity Building: Orbital Asset Deployment & Utilization

**Module:** M3 — Space Economy, Capacity Building & Human Capital
**Estimated Time:** 8 minutes

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Define** strategic management of orbital assets.
2. **Maximize** operational efficiency of current infrastructure.
3. **Plan** the deployment of future orbital assets.

## 🚀 The Hook
> *A satellite in orbit is only as valuable as its strategic placement and utilization. Nations that master orbital asset management win. Nations that don't, waste billions.*

## 📖 Core Content

### 1. The Capacity Building Roadmap
Your notes lay out a step-by-step roadmap. The first steps are:
1. **Orbital Asset Deployment** — Strategic management and positioning.
2. **Space Asset Utilization** — Maximizing operational efficiency.
3. **NigComSat Utilization** — Maximizing usage of NigComSat-1, 2A, 1R.
4. **NigeriaSat Fleet Integration** — NigeriaSat-1, 2, X operations.
5. **Delsat-1 Operations** — Deployment and monitoring.

### 2. Strategic Orbital Positioning
Choosing an orbit is a strategic decision:
- **LEO (Low Earth Orbit)** — Best for EO imaging, low latency.
- **MEO (Medium Earth Orbit)** — Best for navigation constellations.
- **GEO (Geostationary)** — Best for communications, persistent coverage.

### 3. Maximizing Utilization
Deploying assets is only step one. Utilization means:
- Selling excess bandwidth or imagery.
- Coordinating across agencies to avoid duplication.
- Planning for end-of-life replacement before gaps open.

## 📚 Glossary
| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
