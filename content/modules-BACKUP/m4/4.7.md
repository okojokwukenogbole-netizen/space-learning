# Lesson 4.7 — Space-Enabled Election Security & UAV Networks

**Module:** M4 — Space Security, AI & Operational Tactics
**Estimated Time:** 8 minutes

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Address** election threat vectors including cyber warfare.
2. **Analyze** tactical UAV networks in election security.
3. **Execute** hackathon simulations and form OSG.

## 🚀 The Hook
> *Elections are no longer just ballot boxes and polling stations. They are contested in cyberspace, in the air, and increasingly — in space. Securing a modern election requires a multi-domain approach.*

## 📖 Core Content

### 1. Topic 47 in Your Notes
Your notes list: *Space-Enabled Election Security — Addressing election threat vectors including cyber warfare and tactical UAV networks; executing Hackathon simulations, forming OSG (Operational Support Groups), specialist training (Trg), tactical interventions, and securing specialized funding streams.*

### 2. Election Threat Vectors
Modern elections face:
- **Cyber warfare** — Hacking voting systems, disinformation.
- **UAV networks** — Drones disrupting polling or delivering payloads.
- **Communications jamming** — Blocking result transmission.
- **Physical attacks** — On polling stations and officials.
- **Information operations** — Coordinated disinformation campaigns.

### 3. How Space Enables Election Security
Space assets contribute through:
- **Secure communications** — Encrypted transmission of results.
- **Persistent surveillance** — Monitoring hotspots.
- **UAV detection** — Tracking hostile drone activity.
- **Navigation** — GPS for logistics and verification.
- **Resilient networks** — Backup links if terrestrial fails.

### 4. Tactical UAV Networks
Two sides of the same coin:
- **Friendly UAVs** — Border patrol, surveillance, communications relay.
- **Hostile UAVs** — Disruption, payload delivery, reconnaissance.

Space-based Counter-UAS:
- Detects drone signals from orbit.
- Tracks drone swarms.
- Provides early warning to ground defenses.

### 5. Hackathon Simulations
Your notes call for **hackathon simulations** as election security exercises. These involve:
- Red teams simulating cyber and UAV attacks.
- Blue teams defending.
- Real-time response drills.
- Post-exercise lessons learned.

**Goal:** Stress-test the system before election day.

### 6. Operational Support Groups (OSG)
OSGs are rapid-response teams formed to:
- Monitor election infrastructure in real time.
- Coordinate across agencies (military, police, electoral body).
- Escalate threats to decision-makers.
- Deploy tactical interventions if needed.

### 7. Specialist Training (Trg)
Personnel must be trained in:
- Space-based ISR tasking.
- Cyber defense basics.
- UAV counter-operations.
- Multi-agency coordination.
- Legal frameworks for domestic space use.

### 8. Funding Streams
Election security funding may come from:
- National security budgets.
- Electoral commission allocations.
- International partners (with strings).
- Public-private partnerships.
