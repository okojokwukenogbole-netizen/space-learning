Lesson 1.3 — Sovereign Pipelines & Indigenous Capabilities
Learning Objectives:

Identify the components of a sovereign space pipeline.

Explain why home-grown specifications, design, and manufacture matter.

Assess the requirements for independent launch capability.

Key Terms: Sovereign Pipeline, Home-Grown Specs, Independent Launch, Indigenous AIT.

Quiz:

What is the essential first step in a sovereign pipeline?
A) Buying foreign satellites B) Home-grown specifications C) Hosting foreign bases D) Signing treaties
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes explicitly state "Essential need for home-grown specifications."

What does AIT stand for?
A) Assembly, Integration, and Testing B) Advanced Intelligence Technology C) Aerospace Institute of Technology D) Anti-Interference Technology
Answer: A. Rationale: AIT is standard space industry terminology for Assembly, Integration, and Testing.

Which is NOT a component of a sovereign pipeline?
A) Design B) Manufacture C) Independent launch D) Outsourcing all R&D
Answer: D. Rationale: Outsourcing R&D undermines sovereignty and is contrary to the "essential need for home-grown" capabilities.
