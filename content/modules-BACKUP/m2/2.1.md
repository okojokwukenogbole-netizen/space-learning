Lesson 2.1 — Satellite Bus Architecture: 3U to 16U & MicroSats
Learning Objectives:

Differentiate between CubeSat (3U–16U) and MicroSat platforms.

Select an appropriate bus size for a given mission.

Evaluate the trade-offs between size, cost, and capability.

Key Terms: CubeSat, 3U–16U, MicroSat, Satellite Bus.

Quiz:

What is the typical size range for CubeSats?
A) 1U–2U B) 3U–16U C) 20U–50U D) 100U+
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes explicitly state "3U to 16U sizes."

What defines a MicroSat?
A) A CubeSat under 3U B) A dedicated micro-satellite platform capability C) A large GEO satellite D) A ground station
Answer: B. Rationale: Your notes describe it as a "dedicated micro-satellite platform."

What is a key trade-off when moving from CubeSat to MicroSat?
A) Lower cost, less capability B) Higher cost, more capability C) Same cost, same capability D) No trade-offs
Answer: B. Rationale: Larger buses generally carry more capable payloads but cost more.
