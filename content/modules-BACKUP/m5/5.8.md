# Lesson 5.8 — Economic & Resource Security

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. **Deploy** space assets for checkmating illegal mining.
2. **Monitor** illegal fishing and resource theft.
3. **Evaluate** the role of space in economic security.

## 📖 Core Content
Your notes state: *"Economic & Resource Security: Deploying space assets for checkmating illegal mining, illegal fishing, and related resource theft."*

This is where space capability directly translates into national wealth protection:

- **Illegal Mining** — EO satellites detect unauthorized extraction activity in remote areas.
- **Illegal Fishing** — AIS + SAR satellites track vessels operating without licenses.
- **Resource Theft** — Persistent surveillance protects oil, gas, and mineral assets.
- **Revenue Recovery** — Data supports prosecution and tax enforcement.

For emerging economies, space-based resource monitoring is not a luxury — it is a direct instrument of economic sovereignty.

## ❓ Check Your Understanding

**Q1.** What specific illegal activities are mentioned in your notes?

**Answer: A.** Rationale: Your notes state "checkmating illegal mining, illegal fishing."

**Q2.** How can space assets combat illegal mining?
A) By providing high-resolution imagery of mining sites B) By sending troops C) By building walls D) By providing food
**Answer: A.** Rationale: EO satellites can detect and monitor illegal mining activities.

**Q3.** Why is resource security important for emerging economies?
A) It protects national revenue and resources B) It is not important C) It only matters to developed countries D) It slows down economic growth
**Answer: A.** Rationale: Resource theft undermines national revenue and economic development.
